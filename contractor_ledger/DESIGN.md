## Brand & Style

This design system is engineered specifically for independent general contractors, trade specialists, and field project managers who demand immediate, uncompromising financial precision. The brand conveys operational stability, utilitarian dignity, and absolute fiscal clarity. It rejects decorative fluff, consumer-grade gamification, and tech-startup jargon in favor of architectural composure and Swiss typographic rigor.

The aesthetic fuses functional minimalism with structural utility:
- **Radical Clarity:** High-contrast data legibility, structured columns, and immediate visual hierarchy prioritize numbers over decoration.
- **Physical Groundedness:** Surfaces mimic high-spec ledger stock and blueprint documentation rather than floating virtual software layers.
- **Dignified Restraint:** Zero gradients, zero decorative illustrations, and no gratuitous micro-interactions. Every line, stroke, and figure serves an explicit arithmetic purpose.

## Colors

The palette relies on high-legibility field neutrals paired with an authoritative, deep forest green accent that signals solvency, fiscal health, and accuracy.

### Architectural Canvas & Text
- **Canvas Root (`#FAFAF8`):** A warm, low-glare near-white canvas engineered to reduce eye strain in direct sunlight and harsh job-site trailer lighting.
- **Surface Elevation (`#FFFFFF`):** Reserved strictly for primary functional cards, inputs, and calculation panels.
- **Primary Text (`#18211F`):** Deep charcoal-slate providing maximum contrast against white and warm canvas backgrounds without the harsh, vibrating edge of pure `#000000`.
- **Secondary Text (`#606B68`):** Muted slate for peripheral context, unit specifiers, and input labels.
- **Structural Rule (`#E5E8E5`):** Crisp 1px boundary line used to segment ledger columns, rows, and inputs.

### Action & Functional Signals
- **Primary Brand / Action (`#176B53`):** Deep forest green applied to primary commitments, action states, and key calculation totals.
- **Primary Action Hover (`#10523F`):** Darkened green state for pointer hover and active states.
- **Soft Accent Tint (`#ECF5F0`):** Low-saturation mint tint used for selection states, row highlights, and active parameter pills.
- **Error / Deficit (`#B44545`):** Controlled crimson used exclusively for cost overruns, margin deficits, and validation failures.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy enforces clear functional boundaries between structural headings, analytical labels, and computational figures:

- **Headlines (Manrope):** Geometric, solid, and tightly tracked. Used for section titles, estimator stages, and ledger categories. Manrope's confident vertical stems deliver structural authority without industrial harshness.
- **Body & Tabular Figures (Inter):** Inter handles all descriptive text, form inputs, dynamic tallies, line-item valuations, and statistical data. All numerical outputs must enable OpenType tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums; font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1, "cv05" 1;`) to guarantee uniform vertical alignment across currency columns and mathematical formulas.
- **Labels (Inter):** Small labels utilize medium-to-semibold weights with uppercase micro-tracking (`+0.04em`) to serve as immediate visual anchors above form fields.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in a strict 8px structural grid rhythm with a secondary 4px sub-rhythm reserved for internal input elements and label locks.

### Grid & Composition
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column structured layout with an asymmetric split: a primary 7-column or 8-column calculation workbench on the left, locked against a 4-column or 5-column live summary rail on the right. Maximum content bound is 1200px.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column fluid layout with 1.5rem gutters. Summary rails dock beneath calculation inputs while retaining full desktop column structures.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column single-stack flow. The primary output ledger pins dynamically to the screen header or top of the viewport stack. Bottom navigation bars are strictly prohibited to maintain maximum usable vertical data height.

### Spacing Discipline
- Maintain uniform `1rem` (16px) margins between consecutive field modules within a single tier.
- Major ledger groupings are separated by `2rem` (32px).
- Internal element padding strictly matches `space-sm` (8px) or `space-md` (16px).

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids floating cards, soft multi-layer drop shadows, and simulated lighting models. Spatial depth is established strictly through surface tone delineation and calibrated linear boundaries:

- **Zero-Shadow Default:** Cards, panels, and tables exist on the planar surface delineated solely by `1px solid #E5E8E5`.
- **Level 0 (Canvas):** Tone `#FAFAF8`. Base operational layer.
- **Level 1 (Panels & Surfaces):** Tone `#FFFFFF`. Sharp border `1px solid #E5E8E5`. No drop shadow.
- **Level 2 (Active Focus & Modals):** Drop shadows are permitted only on floating contextual menus and full-screen confirmation modals, restricted to a razor-thin structural shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(24, 33, 31, 0.06), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(24, 33, 31, 0.08)`.
- **Card Nesting Rule:** Cards within cards are forbidden. To group sub-items inside a parent card, use alternating subtle row striping or horizontal `1px solid #E5E8E5` rules.

## Shapes

The geometric form factor balances crisp architectural drafts with ergonomic utility:
- **Consistent Radius:** Primary input containers, primary result blocks, buttons, and panels share an exact `8px` (`0.5rem`) corner radius.
- **Micro Radii:** Checkboxes, badge indicators, and parameter toggles utilize `4px` (`0.25rem`).
- **No Bubble/Pill Elements:** Over-rounded pills (`9999px`) are forbidden for buttons and standard inputs. Only compact numeric indicator tags (e.g., markup percentages) may feature rounded forms when explicitly constrained to sub-20px vertical heights.

## Components

### 1. Form Inputs & Number Fields
- **Container:** Minimum height of 44px for immediate touch target reliability. Clean `#FFFFFF` fill with `1px solid #E5E8E5` boundary. 
- **Focus State:** `1px solid #176B53` accompanied by an inner/outer non-blurry ring `0 0 0 1px #176B53`.
- **Prefix / Suffix Locks:** Fixed-width static currency symbols (`$`) and percentages (`%`) rendered in `#606B68`, mechanically segregated from dynamic values by a hairline divider.
- **Labels:** Set at 14px (`body-md`) with 600 weight directly above the input container, accompanied by `space-xs` (4px) vertical distance.

### 2. Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#176B53` background with pure `#FFFFFF` typography (`font-weight: 600`), 44px height, 8px corner radius. Hover state switches immediately to `#10523F`.
- **Secondary / Outline:** `#FFFFFF` fill, `1px solid #E5E8E5`, `#18211F` text. Hover state switches surface tone to `#FAFAF8` with border transition to `#606B68`.
- **Destructive:** Bordered or tinted `#B44545` state with crisp warning iconography.

### 3. Financial Breakdown Bar (Cost vs. Margin)
- A unified horizontal split meter with 8px radius and an explicit 12px or 16px height.
- **Cost Allocation:** Base fill using `#606B68` or deep slate tint `#2E3A36`.
- **Profit / Margin Allocation:** Deep forest green `#176B53`.
- Paired immediately with a dual tabular key detailing exact dollar values and relative percentage margins.

### 4. Tabular Data Lists & Ledger Tables
- Strict 48px minimum row height for clear row-level thumb verification on mobile devices.
- Subtle alternating row backgrounds using `#FAFAF8` or simple bottom border dividers (`1px solid #E5E8E5`).
- Right-aligned tabular numerical data; left-aligned line-item titles.

### 5. Checkboxes & Radio Selectors
- Rigid 20px x 20px squares (checkboxes) and circles (radios) with `1.5px solid #E5E8E5`.
- Selected state turns background to `#176B53` with crisp white SVG checkmarks. No elastic or bouncy animation loops.

### 6. Summary Panels (Results Displays)
- Prominent white card bounded by `1px solid #E5E8E5`.
- Hero output displays total contract estimate using `numeric-hero` typography (`Inter`, bold, tabular numbers, tight tracking).
- Direct secondary breakdowns (Labor, Materials, Overhead, Net Margin) displayed in a 2x2 grid with small uppercase labels (`label-sm`).
